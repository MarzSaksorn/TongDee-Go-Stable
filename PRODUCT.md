# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Thai travelers exploring Phichai and Uttaradit, especially visitors who want local culture, safe movement, and practical trip support on a phone while planning or already out in the area. Secondary users include local taxi providers, marketplace sellers, and admins who keep local services discoverable and trustworthy.

## Product Purpose

Tong Dee Go helps travelers discover Phichai through AI-assisted itineraries, map-based place discovery, cultural storytelling, safety support, local taxi access, OTOP marketplace browsing, and journey records such as passports and check-ins. Success means travelers can confidently choose where to go, understand the local context, move safely, and support community operators without the app feeling generic or bureaucratic.

## Brand Personality

Warm, safe, local. The product should feel like a trusted local travel buddy: friendly and culturally rooted, but still clear, reliable, and task-focused when users need directions, safety, bookings, or admin flows.

## Anti-references

Do not make the app feel like a generic tourism template with interchangeable attraction cards, stock travel language, or placeless blue-and-white polish. Do not make it feel like a government portal: avoid bureaucratic stiffness, form-heavy dead ends, and overly official tone. Do not over-index on decorative AI sparkle at the expense of trust, legibility, and real trip utility.

## Design Principles

1. Local proof over generic polish: every major surface should make Phichai, Uttaradit, local operators, or Thai travel context feel specific.
2. Safety is a first-class affordance: solo-travel confidence, emergency access, location awareness, and clear fallback states matter as much as discovery.
3. Plan in motion, not in theory: optimize for thumb-friendly decisions, quick route comparisons, and readable content during an active trip.
4. Friendly without becoming frivolous: keep buddy-like warmth and encouragement, but let standard controls, clear hierarchy, and honest loading/error states carry trust.
5. Community value is visible: marketplace, taxi, cultural stories, and local events should show how travelers support real local people.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast and interaction states. Preserve Thai-first readability with comfortable line height, tappable mobile controls, and readable placeholder/help text. Support reduced motion for animated safety, loading, and transition effects. Do not rely on color alone for status, safety, selected states, or errors; pair color with labels, icons, or structure.
